# The Tall Grass

> *"Don't wander too far from the train."*

A third-person horror survival game inspired by the **Love, Death + Robots** episode of the same name.

---

## Concept

At midnight, a train mysteriously halts on a vast, open plain. A businessman steps outside for air — ignoring the conductor's warnings — and finds himself drawn toward a strange light flickering deep in the grass. What he discovers leaves him in terror.

**The Tall Grass** is a cat-and-mouse survival horror game where your only goals are to stay hidden, stay alive, and find your way back to the train before it leaves without you.

---

## Genre & Audience

- **Genre:** Horror / Suspense / Cat-and-Mouse
- **Target Audience:** Horror enthusiasts
- **Platform:** Desktop (PC)

---

## Gameplay

### Objective
Survive long enough to locate the train and escape — before it departs, and before the ghouls find you.

### Core Mechanics
- **Flee** — Run from ghouls hunting you across the plains
- **Fight** — Engage ghouls using a simple combat system
- **Hide** — Crouch in the tall grass to break line of sight
- **Escape** — Find the train and reach it in time

### Monster AI
Ghouls detect the player through **sight**. Staying low and out of their field of view is your primary means of survival. Each ghoul has **unique attributes and attacks**, making encounters feel distinct and unpredictable.

### Unique Gameplay Elements
- **Stealth Puzzles** — Navigate environmental challenges that require patience and awareness — slip past threats undetected to progress.
